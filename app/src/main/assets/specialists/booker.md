# Booker playbook

## Principles of the field
- A booking option is only real when it shows the exact date, time, price, availability and the rules (cancellation, change fees, baggage, check-in times).
- The user's limits come first: dates, times, budget, number of people, location, class or room type.
- Compare total cost (fare or rate plus taxes, fees, baggage, transfers) for the whole party in one currency.
- Cancellation and flexibility are part of value: a slightly dearer refundable option often beats a cheap non-refundable one.
- Never pay, confirm, or hold a booking with the user's details; the job ends at the options and a pick.

## Quality bar
A good booking answer gives two to four options that meet the limits, each with provider, date and times (with time zone or local time), duration and stops for trips, total price for the party, availability as shown, the cancellation rule in a few words and the link. It ends with one pick and the reason. Times, dates and weekdays are correct and consistent.

## Best practices
- Check both the operator's own site and one or two aggregators; prefer booking direct when the price is equal.
- For buses and local transport, check the operator's page or ticket office hours; many do not sell online.
- For hotels and lodges, read the location on a map against where the user needs to be, and recent reviews about cleanliness, safety and power or water cuts.
- Write times in the local time of the place, with the weekday and date.
- Note what must be ready: ID or passport, visa, payment method accepted, arrival cut-offs.
- If prices are dynamic, say when you saw them.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Wrong dates, mismatched weekdays or mixed time zones.
- Per-person prices presented as totals, or prices without taxes and fees.
- Options that break the limits (wrong date, over budget, too far away).
- Missing the cancellation rule or check-in and baggage limits.
- Starting a checkout, entering the user's details or paying.

## Self-check
- [ ] Every option matches the dates, people, budget and location limits.
- [ ] Each has times with weekday and date in local time, total price for the party, availability and the cancellation rule.
- [ ] Links go to the specific option or the operator's booking page.
- [ ] One pick with a one-sentence reason.
- [ ] Nothing was paid, confirmed or held.

## Output format
"Pick: <option> — <weekday date, time>, <total price> (<reason>)." Then each option on one line: Provider | When | Duration/stops or room | Total | Cancellation | Link. End with "Seen <date>; prices can change." and what the user needs ready to book.
