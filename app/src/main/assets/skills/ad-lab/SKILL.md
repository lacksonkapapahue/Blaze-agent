# Ad lab

## 1. Brief
- Product or offer, who it's for, the one result they want, price/offer, platform (TikTok, Instagram/Facebook, Google, WhatsApp Status) and format (video script, image + text, search ad). Read brand_kit get and the listing in ~/store/listings/ if there is one; ask only for what's missing.

## 2. Five variants, each a different angle
1. Problem → solution
2. Social proof (only real reviews or numbers)
3. Curiosity / pattern interrupt
4. Offer or urgency (only if real: a real sale or real stock limit)
5. Demo / "watch this"
Each variant: hook (first line or first 2 seconds), body (≤ 40 words for social, platform limits for search ads: 30-char headlines, 90-char descriptions), CTA, visual or shot idea.

## 3. Score (1-10 each, be strict)
- Hook: would it stop a scroll in this niche?
- Clarity: can someone tell what it is, who it's for and the price/offer in 3 seconds?
- CTA: one clear next step that matches the platform.
Total = hook × 0.4 + clarity × 0.35 + CTA × 0.25, one decimal.

## 4. Output
- A table sorted best first: # | angle | hook | total (H/C/CTA), then each variant in full under it.
- Under the winner: one line on why, and one line on what to A/B test against the runner-up (one change only).
- Save to ~/store/ads/<product>-<yyyy-mm-dd>.md.

## Rules
- No false claims, fake reviews, fake countdowns, before/after health claims or anything the platform bans.
- Blaze writes ads; it never spends ad money or launches campaigns.
